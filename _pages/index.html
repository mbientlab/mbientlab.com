MbientLab Research and Development BoardS

Gather sensor data with our light, ultra-small wireless development boards:

MMRL

Low-energy 2.4 GHz wireless communication with an ARM Cortex M4 microcontroller, rechargeable battery, accelerometer, gyroscope, magnetometer and sensor fusion.

MMS

Low-energy 2.4 GHz wireless communication with an ARM Cortex M4 microcontroller, rechargeable battery, temperature, accelerometer, gyroscope, pressure and ambient light sensor.

GATHER SENSOR DATA IN SECONDS

These small boards can be attached to the body, animals, or objects to gather sensor data:

ACCELEROMETER

Measures Acceleration

GYROSCOPE

Measure Angular Velocity

TEMPERATURE

Measures the ambient Temperature

LIGHT

Measures Luminosity

PRESSURE

Measures atmospheric pressure, used in forecasting the weather and determining altitude.

MAGNETOMETER

Measures Magnetic Forces and is generally used for orientation measurements and as a compass.

MOTION

Measures quaternions and Euler angles using the Bosch Sensor Fusion Kalman filter algorithm.
