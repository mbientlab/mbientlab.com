--- title: Troubleshooting permalink: /troubleshooting/ layout: default description: Checklists, resets and warranty information for MetaSensors. topics: - id: general icon: check title: General items intro: A checklist to make sure your MetaSensor is working. image: Debug-1.jpg items: - The LEDs on the board do not turn on automatically. - LEDs are not an indicator of your MetaSensor's status. - Make sure Bluetooth is on. Check your phone settings. - Delete the MetaWear app and download the latest version. - Check whether the battery is puffy or too low. - Do not force open the case, as this may damage your board. - id: wake icon: zap title: Wake it up intro: All MetaSensors ship in sleep mode to conserve battery. image: Debug-3.jpg items: - Plug it in to wake it up. - You can also press the button to wake it up. - It can take up to 40 seconds for a device to connect to your phone the first time. Be patient. - Do not use your phone's Bluetooth menu to connect to the sensor. Use our apps or APIs only. - id: charge icon: battery title: Keep it charged intro: It's important to keep your MetaSensor charged. image: Debug-4.png items: - Charge compatible devices using a micro-USB cable and AC adapter. - Charge your device for a minimum of 40 minutes. - Use a UL certified cable and adapter to charge your MetaSensor. - The USB port is for power only. It does not transfer data (except on the MMS). - Leave it plugged in while you debug or troubleshoot. - id: update icon: refresh title: Update it intro: It's important to keep your MetaSensor updated. image: Debug-5.jpg items: - Connect to your device using the MetaWear app and update the firmware. - If you have an issue, run a diagnostic scan and send us the results on the forum. - If your device is in MetaBoot mode, wait 60 seconds for it to return to normal. - If your device is stuck in MetaBoot, try to update its firmware. - If it is still stuck in MetaBoot, the problem may be your smartphone, since phones cache the MetaBoot state even when the device is working properly. Try a different smartphone. - id: soft-reset icon: power title: Perform a soft reset intro: How to reset your device if you cannot connect to it. image: Debug-2.png ordered: true items: - Plug in the micro-USB cable or insert the coin-cell battery while pressing down the button on the device. - Release the button only once the USB cable or battery is fully inserted (the device should be charging or powered). - The device resets for 60 seconds. - After waiting 1 minute, connect to your device using the app. - If that fails, consider deleting the app and doing a fresh install. - id: hard-reset icon: alert title: Perform a hard reset intro: If your device is unresponsive after the previous steps, you can perform a hard reset. warning: Only do this when our team recommends it. This step is for professionals only, may damage the board, and may void the warranty. image: Debug-7.png ordered: true items: - Use conductive material such as a wire or tweezers to short any PWR and GND pins on the board while the device is powered (preferably plugged in). - A dimly flashing LED during the shorting process indicates success. Short the pins for 1 ms. - Try connecting again. - id: more icon: target title: Additional checks intro: More to check if you still have issues with your MetaSensor. image: metamotionc6.jpg items: - Try to detect the MetaSensor with a Bluetooth scanner app such as nRF Connect from Nordic (iOS or Android). - You can also check whether your MetaSensor is advertising with your phone's Bluetooth scanner. - If the MetaSensor is advertising, it is working. The issue is with the app, APIs, or other software. - Try a different device. If your iPhone is not working, try an iPad or Android phone instead. - Inspect the unit for physical damage or defects. Do not open the case unless our team has asked you to. - id: warranty icon: shield title: Warranty intro: We offer a 30-day limited warranty. items: - MetaSensors have a 30-day limited warranty (free DOA replacement or repair). Some exchanges are allowed. - Devices out of warranty or returned without an RMA number will not be accepted. - If you have an issue with your MetaSensor during use, report it on our community forum. All issues must be reported and documented there. - On the forum, please submit a diagnostic report, photos, and videos of your issue. --- {% include page-hero.html eyebrow="Support" title="Troubleshooting" lead="Most issues come down to sleep mode, charge or firmware.
